Builder first.
Student always.

I’m Miguel Riovaldez, a BS Information Technology student at Mapúa University in the Philippines. I learn best by turning concepts into working software, server systems, databases, and web experiences.

My coursework gives me the fundamentals—software development, databases, computer systems, and networking. Personal and collaborative projects are where I pressure-test those fundamentals and learn how the pieces behave outside an exercise.

I’ve built a Windows desktop application backed by SQLite, worked on Java-based server development and configuration, supported a modded multiplayer server, and designed this portfolio from scratch with vanilla web technologies.

I’m working toward full-stack development, but I’m equally interested in the systems around an application: data, infrastructure, deployment thinking, debugging, and the details that make a build usable.
